New Publication: Designing Singing Syllabi with Virtual Avatars

My new manuscript, Designing singing syllabi with virtual avatars: AI-assisted syllabus reauthoring, has been published in Open Scholarship of Teaching and Learning.

Published: September 10, 2026 · Vol. 5 No. 2 · Author: Xinxing Wu

This work explores an AI-assisted workflow for transforming a traditional syllabus into a musical, video-based, and virtual-avatar-enhanced learning artifact. The singing syllabus is intended as a supplement to the formal written syllabus and as a more engaging way to introduce students to course information.
